开始闯关

1选择题：long 的比较级是 ______.

知识点：一般 + er

2填空题：Those dresses are ______ than these ones.（更好看的）

知识点：比较级

3选择题：Amy is ______ than both of us.

知识点：比较级 + than

4填空题：That's the ______ dinosaur in this hall.

知识点：最高级

5选择题：—How heavy are you? —I'm ______.

知识点：How heavy 问体重

6填空题：Look! The girl is ______ kungfu.

知识点：Look 表示正在进行

7选择题：My father ______ to music last night.

知识点：过去时间

8填空题：She ______ to the cinema with her friends yesterday.

知识点：go 的过去式

9选择题：Did you ______ a film last Saturday?

知识点：Did + 原形

10填空题：I ______ at home all day yesterday.

知识点：stay 的过去式

11选择题：go camping 的过去式是 ______.

知识点：go -> went

12填空题：I ______ lots of pictures at the beach.

知识点：take 的过去式

13选择题：—How did you go there? —We went there by ______.

知识点：交通工具

14填空题：It looks ______ a mule.

知识点：描述看起来像什么

15选择题：There ______ no computer or Internet in my time.

知识点：no computer or Internet 作整体

16填空题：There ______ no gyms before.

知识点：复数 gyms

17选择题：Before, I ______ quiet. Now, I'm active.

知识点：Before 过去

18填空题：People couldn't ______ the Internet before.

知识点：couldn't + 原形

19选择题：Now I ______ cycling every day.

知识点：Now + 一般现在时

20填空题：We are all ______ now.

知识点：Unit 4 核心句
